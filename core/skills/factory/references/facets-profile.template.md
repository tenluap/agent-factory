# FACETS profile

Copy to `${FACTORY_HOME:-~/.factory}/facets-profile.md` and fill in every field. `facets-advisor` judges Capital, Expertise and Time against these values.

- `hours_per_week`: hours you can spend on it each week, e.g. `8`
- `capital_at_risk`: money you can lose without harm, with currency, e.g. `500 USD`
- `skills`: skills you can use today, comma-separated, e.g. `C#, Avalonia, technical writing`
- `face_comfort`: `yes`, `no` or `optional`, whether you are willing to show your face publicly
- `income_goal`: amount and horizon, e.g. `2000 USD/month within 12 months`
- `current_assets`: audience, products, contacts or distribution you already have, or `none`
